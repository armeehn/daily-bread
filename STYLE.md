✎ Answer each; each answer becomes a rule (V1, V2, …) with an example in your
words.

- Who is speaking: *we*, *I*, nobody? Who are we talking to?
- How formal? Contractions? Slang, and whose?
- Humour: where it belongs, and where it never does.
- Anger: allowed? How does it sound when it is right?
- Words and phrases you never want to see. Words you love.

## P — People, place and language about us

✎ These carry real consequences; decide them deliberately.

- How we name the land and the Nation(s) we are on, and who we asked.
- Names and pronouns (a default when unknown; never a deadname).
- Identity words: whose words, and when they belong in a piece at all.
- Young writers: bylines, how much we edit them, consent.
- Health, crisis and safety information: how it is checked and dated.
- The funder: how Riposte Laboratories is named, and where its voice may appear
  (BRAND.md already says it is credited as the funder, not the voice).

## M — Mechanics

Rules marked **fixed** come from how the magazine is built and should stay
unless the build changes. The rest are ✎ decisions.

**M1. fixed · Capitals come from the design, not the keyboard.** Type headings
and labels in normal case; the stylesheet sets them in capitals. Typed capitals
break search and the fifteen translations (BRAND.md).

**M2. fixed · Formatting is bold, italic and links only.** The pages keep `<b>`,
`<i>` and `<a>` and escape everything else; the studio's B / I / Link strip
writes them. Anything fancier will not survive.

**M3. fixed · Fit the page.** Every section's word limit is shown in the studio
(*All fields*, beside the section name). It is measured on the page, so it
moves when the layout does.

✎ **Spelling:** Canadian (colour, centre)? US? Something else?

✎ **Dashes:** em dash spaced or closed, or avoided? (The translators are
already told never to add one their language would not use.)

✎ **Quotation marks:** curly or straight?

✎ **Numbers and dates:** figures or words, and the date format for listings and
for prose.

✎ **Issue numbers:** №1, No. 1, #1, Issue One?

✎ **Serial comma:** yes or no?

✎ **Swearing, content notes, spoilers:** allowed, required, never?

## The submissions desk

✎ What the desk may do with a draft. Pick one and delete the others:

- **Point only:** name the rule (by number) and quote the sentence; the writer
  decides what to change.
- **Suggest:** as above, plus one suggested rewrite per note, marked as a
  suggestion.
- **Rewrite with consent:** it may return an edited draft, and nothing is used
  until the writer says yes.

In every case the desk quotes the writer's own words back, cites this document
by rule number, and never changes the meaning of what was said.
