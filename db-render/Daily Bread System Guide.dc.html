01 · THE IDEA
An issue is a stack of independent A5 pages (561×794). Each page is one template component with its content passed in as props — like LaTeX arguments. You never place a box, set a margin, or pick a font. Reordering the magazine = reordering the blocks. Any prop you leave blank falls back to №1's real content, so a half-filled issue always renders whole.

Three ways to work: 1) duplicate the №2 Starter and fill its holes; 2) select any page in the preview and edit its props in the Tweaks panel; 3) tell Claude what a page should say — or ask it to add, swap, or reshuffle pages.

05 · HOUSE RULES
▸ Pages don't grow. A5 is fixed; overflow clips like a real magazine. Trim the body until nothing clips — about 3 paragraphs per text page.
▸ Slugs are unique. Two pages sharing a slug share dropped photos. v2 uses v2-pNN; the starter uses i2-pNN.
▸ Folios are manual. Reordering is free, but renumber folio props before print (or ask Claude to renumber the file).
▸ The Wall ships as three pages: piece 01, piece 02, artist bio — keep the two art pages adjacent so they rip out as a pair.
▸ Print: when an issue is locked, ask Claude to regenerate the print + saddle-stitch booklet files from it — pagination is derived from block order.
